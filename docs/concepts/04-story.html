How much does it cost, and how do I pay?

The 3-Month Brand Sprint is $24,000. You can pay in three monthly payments of $8,000, or 50% to start and 50% at the end of month 3. Work starts when the first payment is in.

What if we finish early?

The fee covers the full sprint, even if we wrap sooner. You are buying the engagement, not a month-by-month menu.

What happens in month 3?

We finish and hand off: tie up loose ends, hand over your files, and start on your first one or two touch points. If you continue, the ongoing work starts here too. Month 3 is not a full list of deliverables; it closes out the identity work properly.

Do I have to continue after 3 months?

No. After the sprint you can part ways, or stay on. Continuing is optional.

What does month to month vs. a 6-month commitment cost?

Month to month is $6,000/month. A 6-month commitment is $5,000/month, a lower rate for a long-term partnership. Either side can end either option with 30 days' written notice. Nothing further is owed. Payments already made are not refunded.

What if a payment is late?

Work pauses until the payment comes in. The payment date and the schedule stay the same, so paused days are not added back. Fees do not change.

What if I need more time to give feedback?

We plan around replies within 3 to 5 business days. If a reply takes longer, the timeline stays the same and less time remains, so we fit the scope to the time left or add time at $300/hour. Fees and payment dates stay the same either way.

Who signs off?

One named representative gives written sign-off (email is fine) at the end of each step. The next step starts after that.

Can we skip to design, or ask for extras early?

No design until the message is signed off. Extra requests get scheduled into the right step, or done at $300/hour.

Who owns the work?

Final approved work becomes yours once the sprint is paid in full. Unused concepts and drafts stay with the studio. We may show the work in our portfolio.

Are payments refundable?

Payments are non-refundable.

What are your hours?

We work 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Closed on Shabbos and Jewish holidays. Same-day turnaround is not part of the service.

Do you use AI?

We utilize AI as much as possible to the extent that it helps us get a better result for your message and identity. We refuse to simply use AI to cut corners on turnaround time and mediocre results just because the client is OK with it.
